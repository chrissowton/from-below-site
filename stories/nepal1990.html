Nepal's People's Movement of 1990

Rival parties, from liberals to communists, joined forces against Nepal's absolute monarchy. After 49 days of strikes and protests, the king lifted the ban on political parties.

When
18 February – 8 April 1990
Where
Asan, Kathmandu, Nepal
Facts checked
2026-11-15

See it on the map →

01

What is the background to this event?

In 1960, King Mahendra banned political parties and created the partyless "Panchayat" system, with power in the king's hands.1

  • King Birendra continued this system from 1972.1
  • In a 1980 referendum, multi-party democracy narrowly lost.1
  • In 1989, the Nepali Congress and the United Left Front, a coalition of communist parties, joined to campaign for democracy.1

03

What happened?

The People's Movement began on 18 February 1990, now Democracy Day in Nepal.1

  • The government arrested party leaders the day before.1
  • In late February, police fired on a demonstration in Bhaktapur, killing 12 people.1
  • Students marched, hundreds were arrested and injured, and general strikes (bandhs) spread across the country.1
  • After the army killed protesters in Patan in early April, around 200,000 people marched in Kathmandu.1
  • Police shot dozens of people over several days.1
  • On 8 April 1990, the king lifted the ban on political parties.1

04

What happened next?

  • Constitution: a new constitution in November 1990 made Nepal a constitutional monarchy.1
  • Unity: the alliance led to the formation of the Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Marxist–Leninist).1
  • 2006: a second People's Movement in 2006 led to the end of the monarchy in 2008.
05

What do others say?

At the time

The palace presented the movement as a threat to national unity; protesters demanded multi-party democracy.1

Debates since

  • Unfinished: many argue the 1990 settlement left too much power with the king, leading to the Maoist insurgency and the 2006 movement.
  • Violence: some protesters attacked government property; police and the army shot and killed dozens of protesters.1
