The Paika Rebellion

Odisha's peasant militia had lost their land and status under East India Company rule. In 1817 they rose, sacking Company offices and spreading revolt across much of Odisha.

When
1817
Where
Khurda, Odisha, India
Facts checked
2026-11-16

See it on the map →

01

What is the background to this event?

The Paikas were a peasant militia: they farmed in peacetime and fought for the rulers of Odisha in war.1

  • In 1803, the East India Company conquered Odisha, and the Raja of Khurda was later dethroned.1
  • The Paikas lost the rent-free lands they had held for their military service, and many were forced to give their land to absentee landlords.1
  • The Company raised the price of salt through taxes, and demanded that taxes be paid in silver instead of cowrie shells.1

03

What happened?

In 1817, Khond people from the hills joined the Paikas, led by Bakshi Jagabandhu, against the Company.1

  • They looted and burned the police station and post office at Banpur.1
  • They marched on Khurda, which the Company abandoned, and sacked the administrative buildings and treasury.1
  • Another group killed Indian officials of the Company at Lembai.1
  • The rebels used Lord Jagannath as a symbol of Odia unity, and the revolt spread across most of Odisha.1
  • The Company sent troops, and fought for years in the jungles of Khurda.1

04

What happened next?

  • Jagabandhu surrendered in 1825, and died a prisoner in Cuttack in 1829.1
  • An inquiry: the Company set up a commission into the causes of the rebellion, but its revenue policies remained unchanged.1
05

What do others say?

At the time

Company officials saw the Paikas as dangerous and wanted them reduced to ordinary peasants.1

Debates since

  • First war of independence? In 2017, Odisha asked for it to be recognised as India's first war of independence; in 2021 the national government declined.1
  • Violence: rebels killed Company officials and burned buildings; Company forces hunted down rebels for years afterwards.1

07

How is it remembered?

  • India marked the bicentenary in 2017 with celebrations and a stamp.
08

Why does it matter?

The Paika Rebellion was one of the earliest armed risings against British rule in India.

  • It shows how colonial land and tax policies hit ordinary people.
